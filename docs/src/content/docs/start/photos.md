Waypoint reads each family member’s Immich library and matches photos to trips by when they were taken and then by where: a photo taken during a trip’s dates near one of its bookings belongs to that trip. The pictures stay in Immich; Waypoint keeps only when and where each photo was taken and which trip it matched. The design is in [docs/PHOTOS.md](https://github.com/AnthonyPluth/Waypoint/blob/main/docs/PHOTOS.md).

Photos need sign-in: without it (local mode) there are no libraries, and the photo settings answer as if they did not exist.

## Connecting a library

Each member connects their own Immich library under **Settings → Photos**, with the address of the Immich server and an API key made for Waypoint.

1. In Immich, open **Account Settings → API Keys** and create a key for Waypoint. Give it these permissions, and no more: `asset.read`, `asset.view`, `user.read`, `map.read` and `asset.download` (originals are shown through Waypoint, never straight from Immich). An older Immich without per-key permissions needs `all`. The form lists the same permissions under the key field.
2. In Waypoint, choose **Connect** beside **Connect an Immich library**. Under **Whose library**, keep yourself, or choose a person without a sign-in of their own whose library you manage (below). Enter the **Immich address** (scheme, host and port, such as `https://immich.example.com` or `http://immich.local:2283`; a plain `http://` address is accepted only on your own network) and the **API key** (**Show** reveals what you typed), and choose **Test**. Waypoint asks Immich who the key belongs to and shows the name (“Key works. It belongs to the Immich user …”), saving nothing. If Immich refuses the key, or Waypoint can’t reach the address, the form says so under the fields and nothing is saved.
3. Choose **Save**. It stays off until **Test** has passed for the address and key exactly as typed, so changing either means testing again. Waypoint checks them once more, and keeps the key encrypted with `WAYPOINT_SECRET_KEY`, like a Gmail connection’s token. The key is never shown again: not on the page, in a reply, a log or an error; a backup carries it encrypted.

Each library card shows its person, its host and a status: **Up to date**, **Not synced yet**, **Syncing**, **Not up to date** (the last sync stopped short, with the reason under the host, such as “Some of your library couldn’t be read” or Immich asking Waypoint to slow down), **Paused**, **Key refused** (Immich didn’t accept the key, or it lacks a permission), **Key lost** (Waypoint can no longer unlock the saved key, so enter it again with **Change key**) or **Can’t reach Immich**. On a library you connected, the card also shows its last sync and how many photos it has on how many trips, and its buttons; a library someone else connected shows only the person, the host and **Connected**, so a library’s size says nothing about trips you can’t see.

- **Sync now** starts a read of the library; while the page is open the card says **Syncing** and updates when the read finishes (a read stops after ten minutes, and the next one carries on).
- **Change key** opens a key field in the card and replaces the key only when the new one belongs to the same Immich account as the old one. A key for a different account is refused: remove the library and connect it again, so one person’s photos can never be re-attributed by swapping keys. On a library whose key Immich refused, **Change key** is the card’s main button.
- **Pause** stops reading the library until **Resume**; **Remove** asks first, then deletes the library and the photo references Waypoint kept from it, so its photos leave every trip for everyone. Nothing changes in Immich. A paused library whose key Waypoint can no longer unlock stays paused, with the message saying so, until a new key is saved with **Change key**: resuming it is refused until then, and pausing it again keeps the message.
- A library connected while its person already shares photos with everyone on some trip starts paused, because that sharing was agreed for the libraries that were reading at the time; resuming it (or any paused library of theirs while such sharing is on) needs a confirmation first, even when it would add no photos: Waypoint counts the photos of that library that would newly join the trips shared with everyone (photos it already shows there are not counted), and resumes only with that confirmation. If those trips or the person’s libraries changed since the count was shown, the resume is refused and the library stays paused, so no library joins a shared trip unseen. Settings can’t ask for that confirmation yet: **Resume** on such a library says so and leaves it paused.

### Who connects whose library

A member who signs in connects only their own libraries, so nobody else can attach a library to them. A person in People who has no sign-in of their own (a child) has one steward: the member who connected their first library, recorded with the child’s sharing settings the moment that library is saved and kept even after their last library is removed, so another member cannot take over by connecting one; the steward connects all of the child’s libraries and shares for them, and another member trying to connect one is refused. The steward’s standing ends with their access (then another member may become steward, starting with nothing shared) or when the child signs in and links the guest entry to themselves: their libraries become their own and the former steward keeps nothing.

### When someone loses access

A library ends with its owner’s access: when the member who connected it can no longer sign in to Waypoint (taken off `OIDC_ALLOWED_EMAILS`, or past their sign-in with `OIDC_ALLOWED_GROUPS`), Waypoint’s hourly sweep deletes the library and the photo references it kept, as it does their Gmail connection.

## What Waypoint reads and keeps

Waypoint reads photos taken during the trips its person is on, and the 12 hours around them, and keeps for each only its Immich id, when and where it was taken and which trip and booking it matched, including photos that turn out not to belong to a trip; never the pictures, file names or camera data. Thumbnails and originals are fetched from Immich when shown and are never written to disk or the database. The Immich address and key never reach the browser, and nothing about a library is reachable from an AI assistant over MCP.

### How pictures reach your browser

A picture is never loaded from Immich directly: the browser asks Waypoint for a photo’s thumbnail, preview or original by Waypoint’s own id, Waypoint checks that you may see that photo (the same check as everywhere else: the trip is yours to see, its person shared their photos on it with you, and it was not removed), and only then asks Immich for it with the library’s key, which stays on the server. A photo you may not see answers as if it did not exist. What comes back is the picture’s bytes with a type Waypoint reads from the bytes themselves, never anything Immich said about it: thumbnails and previews are WebP, JPEG or PNG; originals are shown inline when a browser can display them (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF, MP4, QuickTime and WebM video) and offered as a download otherwise (HEIC, TIFF, RAW, Matroska), and anything else answers as missing. Thumbnails and previews may sit in your browser’s cache, but the browser must ask Waypoint before showing one again, so a photo taken off a trip is gone on the next look; originals are never cached. A video seeks by asking for byte ranges, each checked the same way, and a stream in progress stops within a few seconds of its photo being taken back. When Immich has not made a thumbnail yet, the request fails and the browser can simply try again; when Immich no longer has the photo at all, Waypoint drops its reference. A key Immich refuses marks the library **refused**, and an Immich that does not answer gets a fixed message, never Immich’s own text.

### How a library is read

Waypoint reads each library every six hours (the first read ten minutes after it starts), one library at a time, and only for the trips its person shared their photos on: it asks Immich for the photos taken during each such trip and the 12 hours around it, and after the first read only for the ones Immich changed since, so a location added later in Immich moves the photo to where it was taken. Sharing a trip or putting a photo back reads that trip at once. Once a week a library is read in full, and photos deleted or archived in Immich are dropped; a photo you removed from a trip stays removed. A read that runs long stops after ten minutes and the next one carries on where it stopped. If more photos share one capture second than Immich returns in one answer, Waypoint can’t step past them: the library shows “Some of your library couldn’t be read”, keeps its last sync time, and drops nothing from that trip. Only the library's own photos are kept, never ones a partner shares with it.

A library whose key Immich refuses is marked **refused** and is not read again until its key is changed or tested; one Immich doesn't answer is marked **unreachable** and keeps its last sync time; when Immich asks Waypoint to slow down, the read stops and tries again at the next one. Waypoint's log records only counts for each read, never a photo, a place or a trip.

## Sharing your photos on a trip

Nothing from your library is on a trip until you share it there. On each trip you choose who sees the photos you share: **Only me** (the default) or **Everyone on this trip** (the people on the trip; someone who merely booked it or received a confirmation for it is not one of them). You can change that choice at any time, take single photos off a trip (**Remove from this trip**) and put them back, or stop sharing on a trip altogether; each change takes effect at once, for everyone and everywhere a photo appears (the gallery, the map, the replay, the trip’s counts and summary). Photos you remove stay removed through every later sync, even after you stop and share again or connect the library anew, and are listed for you alone under **Removed from this trip** with **Put back**. Stopping sharing takes every photo of yours off the trip for everyone and keeps your removals for when you share again. Nothing changes in Immich.

**Share on all my trips** is a standing setting in Settings: while it is on, every trip you are on, including ones you join later, is shared with your default choice without asking. Turning it on shares every trip you are on now, including ones you had declined or stopped; a trip you stop while it is on stays stopped. Turning it off changes no trip.

### Photo sharing in Settings

Under the library cards, **Settings → Photos** has a **Photo sharing** section for the person it is about: you, and a person without a sign-in whom you manage (a switch named **Whose sharing** picks between them, and the section says you manage their sharing because they don’t sign in). Nobody sees anyone else’s sharing here.

- **Who sees the photos you share** is the default for new shares, **Only me** or **Everyone on the trip**. Changing it changes no trip that is already shared.
- **Share on all my trips** turns the standing setting on or off. With the default **Only me** it saves at once. With **Everyone on the trip** it first asks “Show your 319 photos to everyone on 6 trips?”, with the count of the photos that would newly join those trips; the confirmation is bound to that count, so if your photos or trips changed since it was counted, Waypoint says so, shows the new count and asks again, and nothing is shared until you confirm the count you see. A count that couldn’t be made (“Couldn’t check your libraries”) can’t be confirmed. Turning the setting off never asks and changes no trip. Changing the default to **Everyone on the trip** while the setting is on asks first, because it applies to trips you join later.
- **Trips with your photos** lists every trip you can see that your libraries have photos from, with its dates, photo count and, for a shared trip, how many you removed (the first three, then **Show more trips**). A shared trip has a select for who sees your photos there and **Stop sharing**. Choosing **Everyone on this trip** asks once (“Show your 169 photos to everyone on this trip?”); choosing **Only me** saves at once, with **Undo** in the message that confirms it; **Stop sharing** asks once and says plainly that your photos leave the trip right away, for everyone. A trip that isn’t shared shows **Not shared** and **Share…**, which opens the choice of who sees them, with **Share on this trip** and **Not now**; it says “Couldn’t check your libraries” instead of a number when the count failed, and then only **Only me** can be shared until it can be counted.

Sharing at the **Everyone on this trip** scope, on one trip or on all, always sends the count it was shown (the count token), so Waypoint can refuse a confirmation whose count is out of date. The same goes for your libraries: a confirmation carries the libraries its count covered, so if you connect, pause or resume a library (on another device, say) before you confirm, Waypoint refuses it, shows the new count and asks again. If Settings can’t reload the list after a change, it says so, with **Try again**, and holds the controls until it can show the list as it is.

Only you can share, remove or stop sharing your own photos. A person without a sign-in of their own (a child) is shared for by the member who connected their library, on the trips that member can see. Nobody else, the trip’s organiser included, can remove or hide someone else’s photo, and nothing on a trip tells anyone that photos they can’t see exist: counts, pins, the map’s extent and the summary only ever count the photos the viewer may see.

## On a trip

A trip page shows the photos shared on it that you may see, in the order they were taken, each with the wall-clock time where it was taken (the time zone comes with the photo when Immich knows it, otherwise from the booking the photo is nearest to) and the booking it is nearest to. A photo with no location of its own is placed from the itinerary (the stay or rental under way when it was taken) and marked as such. A photo taken far from every booking of its trip is still shown, with a flag, until its person accepts, moves or removes it.

The **Photos** section sits between the route map and the bookings, once you are signed in (photos are off in local mode). It opens as a strip of the trip's first photos, with a last tile that says how many more there are; that tile or **See all** opens the gallery in place, grouped by the local day each photo was taken, each day headed with its date, the day of the trip, the places of its bookings and how many photos it has. **Show more days** adds the next days (they also load as you scroll), and **Show fewer** goes back to the strip. A photo far from every booking has an amber **Far** tag, a photo placed from the itinerary a hollow ring, and a video a play mark.

Tap a photo to open the viewer: the photo or the video's still (with **Play video**), the time where it was taken (never converted to your own zone), where it was taken and where that came from, whose library it is from, and **Open original**, which opens the original through Waypoint in a new tab. Move between photos with **Previous photo** and **Next photo**, the arrow keys or a sideways swipe; **Close**, Escape or a swipe down leaves it, back on the photo you opened. A photo that can't be loaded says so, with **Retry**.

With no library connected, a trip that has started points you to **Connect a library in Settings**; while your library has not synced yet the section says so, and a trip with no photos says **No photos on this trip yet.**

For the map, the photos are grouped into clusters: photos taken within a few kilometres of each other form one pin, with its count, its first and last time, and a cover photo. A pin whose every photo was placed from the itinerary is drawn hollow. A very long trip keeps at most 400 pins by merging the smallest into their nearest neighbours.

## Needs a look

A photo Waypoint could not settle waits under **Needs a look** for the person whose library it came from: one taken during two overlapping trips they shared that distance could not tell apart, or one taken far from every booking of the trip it matched. Each is listed with the trips it could belong to, which are only trips its person shared and did not remove it from. The person picks the trip, accepts the match as it is, removes it from its trip, or leaves an unsettled photo off every trip it could belong to; a photo they have placed by hand keeps that trip through every later sync. A photo can only be moved to a trip its person is on and has shared their photos on.

## Who sees what

A trip you cannot see answers as if it did not exist, and so does a photo you may not see. Seeing a trip is not enough to see a photo on it: the photo’s person must have shared their photos on that trip with everyone and you must be one of the trip’s people, or you must be that person (or share for them), and the photo must not have been removed from the trip. The same photo reached through two of a person’s libraries is one photo. Two people on the same trip can therefore see different photos, different counts and different places, and neither is told of the difference. Photos are off without sign-in (local mode), and assistants connected over MCP never reach photos, grants or removals.
