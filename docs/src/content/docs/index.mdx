## What Waypoint is for

A family trip is a dozen confirmation emails, and the one you need at the airport is never the one you can find. Waypoint gathers them for the household: who is flying where and when, which hotel and which rental car, and the confirmation number for each. It also keeps everyone’s frequent-flyer, hotel-loyalty and TSA PreCheck numbers, so anyone in the family can book for anyone else.

Each person sees the trips they’re on or booked. Bookings arrive by [scanning Gmail](/Waypoint/privacy/email-scanning/) read-only on your own server, or you add them by hand.

## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Trips" icon="rocket">
    Flights, stays, rental cars, trains, cruises and parking, with confirmation numbers, travellers, costs and times shown where they happen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gmail scanning" icon="email">
    Read-only, private scanning of booking emails, with a Review queue for what couldn’t be read.
  </Card>
  <Card title="People and loyalty IDs" icon="open-book">
    Members and guests, with their loyalty and Known Traveler numbers, encrypted and masked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Before and during a trip" icon="random">
    Live flight status, check-in reminders, a private calendar feed, and your upcoming trips available offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stats" icon="bars">
    Flights, distance, airlines, hotel nights and the places you’ve been, on a map.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optional AI" icon="star">
    Off by default. A local Ollama or OpenRouter reads the mail Waypoint couldn’t, and assistants like Claude can connect over MCP.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
