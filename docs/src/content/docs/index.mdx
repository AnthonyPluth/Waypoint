## What Waypoint is for

Planning a family trip means a dozen confirmation emails, and the one you need at the airport is never the one you can find. Waypoint gathers them for the household: who is flying where and when, in which seat, which hotel and which rental car, and the confirmation numbers for each. It also remembers everyone’s frequent-flyer, hotel-loyalty and TSA PreCheck (Known Traveler) numbers, so anyone in the family can book for anyone else.

You see only the trips you’re on or that you booked. Bookings arrive by [scanning Gmail privately](/Waypoint/privacy/email-scanning/): read-only, searched on the server, a message is kept, encrypted, only while a review item or a booking needs it.

## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Trips" icon="rocket">
    Flights, stays, rental cars, trains, cruises and parking, with confirmation numbers, travellers and times where they happen.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Gmail scanning" icon="envelope">
    Read-only, private scanning of booking emails, with a Review queue for what couldn’t be read.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Optional AI" icon="star">
    Off by default; a local Ollama, or OpenRouter with zero data retention, suggests a booking you then confirm.
  </Card>
  <Card title="People and loyalty IDs" icon="open-book">
    Household members, guests and their loyalty and Known Traveler numbers, encrypted and masked.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Reminders and offline" icon="random">
    Live flight status, push reminders, a calendar feed, and your current trip available offline.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Stats and assistants" icon="setting">
    Travel stats with a map, and AI assistants (MCP) that read your trips and, if you allow it, change them.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
