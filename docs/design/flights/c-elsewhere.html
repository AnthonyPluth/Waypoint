Where else a flight appears

Mockups for the places a flight shows besides its own card: the next flight on Home, flight rows in the trip list, and the booking on the trip page. Drawn with Waypoint's own tokens (frontend/src/app.css), dark only, at phone width (390 px); on desktop the same blocks keep this layout inside their columns. Every flight, name and airport here is made up.

a Flight cardb Live statusc Elsewhere

What this shows

  1. Home next flight: a small route card with the status pill, the amber times when delayed, the gate and when it was last updated.
  2. Trip list rows: a kind tile, DEN to GJT · EA 214, the time and a status chip. Today's flight shows the time of departure; later flights the date.
  3. The booking on the trip page: the booking card with a progress bar while the flight is in the air.
  4. Tapping any of them opens the live status view (b).
c

Home, list rows and the booking

Three small versions of the card for the places where a full card would crowd the page. They show the same wording, tones and times as a, and all lead to b.

Accessible names

  • Each card or row is one link named with the flight, route, state and the next time: EA 214, Denver to Grand Junction, delayed 35 minutes, now departs 10:15 am. Its destination is the live status view (b).
  • The progress bar on the trip-page booking is a progressbar named Flight progress. The kind tile, plane glyph and mini arc are decorative.
  • The row list is named Flights today. The pill is text; amber times also say delayed in the link name.

Tokens and components

  • Home card: the flight card at mini scale (codes 24px, times 13px Geist Mono, a dotted line with the plane at its progress), a footer with Terminal B · Gate B14 and Updated 4 min ago. Shown for the next flight within a day of departure, and while in the air.
  • Row: KindTile (flight, --kind-flight), name DEN to GJT · EA 214, one meta line, a StatusChip. Today's flight shows its departure time; later ones the date and time.
  • Trip-page booking: the booking card with a one-line progress bar, Departed 9:46 am and Lands 10:52 am.
  • No new token.

Replaces

  • The next-flight line and the FlightStatus block on Home (frontend/src/pages/Upcoming.svelte), the flight rows in the trip list and the flight booking card in Trip.svelte.
  • On desktop these blocks keep this layout: the Home card in the page column, the rows in the trips list, the booking in the 400 px column. The times are wall-clock times at their airports; the zone is added only when it differs from the other end's or the viewer's own.
