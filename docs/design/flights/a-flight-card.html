Flight cards: the flight segment card, eight states

Mockups for the flight segment card on the trip page, drawn with Waypoint's own tokens (frontend/src/app.css). Dark only. Use the toggle to see them at phone width (390 px) or desktop width (1280 px; two cards across in the 960 px content column). Every flight, name and airport here is made up. The frames leave out the app's own navigation to show the card itself.

What this shows

  1. The route is the headline: two large airport codes, a dotted arc and a plane marker for progress. Flight identity top left, total duration top right.
  2. Times sit under their airport as wall-clock times with the zone, never converted; a delay turns them amber with the original struck through above.
  3. Four facts in big type, each marked as from live status (filled blue dot) or from the booking (hollow ring); an unknown value is a dash, not a missing row.
  4. The eight states: scheduled, boarding, delayed, in the air, landed, cancelled, diverted, and scheduled with no live data (no Updated line, and the card says when live status starts).
  5. Replaces the flight view of PassCard.svelte and FlightStatus.svelte on the trip page.

The flight card in eight states

One card for every state. Before departure the facts are Terminal, Gate, Seat, Cabin; in the air and once landed they are Terminal, Gate, Belt, Seat (the arrival side). The status sentence is one plain line. The card opens the live status view (b). On phone the cabin reads Econ; on desktop Economy (an open question).

Accessible names

  • Each card is an article named in one phrase with the flight, route and state: EA 214, Denver to Grand Junction, delayed 35 minutes, now departs 10:15 am.
  • The arc, the plane marker and the Waypoint mark tile are decorative (aria-hidden); the progress is in the sentence (Landing in Grand Junction at 10:52 am, in 38 minutes).
  • Each fact is a term and a value in a dl. The source dot is an image named From live status or From the booking; an unknown value reads Not known yet (the dash is hidden from the reader).
  • A struck-through time reads Originally 9:40 am before the new time. The status pill is text, not colour alone.
  • Updated 4 min ago is plain text between the times; when there is no live data it is not rendered.

Tokens and components

  • Card: rounded-[22px] border border-border bg-card shadow-card, the same as the trip cards. Codes 38px/44px semibold, tracking -0.03em; times and flight number in Geist Mono.
  • Status pill: StatusChip tones: quiet (Scheduled, no live data), info (Boarding, In the air), warn (Delayed, amber --signal-ink times), ok (Landed), bad (Cancelled, Diverted).
  • Mark tile: Waypoint's own mark with the flight's two-letter carrier code in --avatar-1, never an airline logo. Source dots: filled --primary for live status, a hollow --muted-foreground ring for the booking.
  • No new token. Dark only.

Replaces

  • The flight view of frontend/src/lib/components/PassCard.svelte on the trip page and the FlightStatus.svelte block it renders (the Terminal X · Gate Y lines and the Refresh button).
  • Gate, terminal and belt come from the status the app already carries (dep_gate, arr_gate, dep_terminal, arr_terminal); where a value is not held the card shows a dash. Seat and cabin stay the booking's (details).
  • A trip that is over keeps the completed-trip designs (trips README): gate and live status stay hidden there; whether the card stays after landing is an open question in the README.
