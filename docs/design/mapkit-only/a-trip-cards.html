MapKit only: Trip cards

How the Past trips cards show Apple’s map, at phone width (390 px) and desktop width (1280 px; the 960 px content column). Drawn with Waypoint’s tokens (frontend/src/app.css), dark only. Apple’s map is a stylised stand-in (a real tile can’t be committed); the yellow tags are notes for the reader, not part of the app. Every name and place is made up. See the README for the inventory, privacy statement and build plan.

a

Past cards on Apple’s map

Only a Past card with no photos the viewer may see and a route of two or more stops gets a map. The map is Apple’s static snapshot of the trip’s area (option A, decided by the owner on #833); Waypoint draws the route and the stops on top in its own map tokens, using Apple’s stored road for a drive when there is one. While the image loads the card shows its kinds tile with the icons at half strength; the image fades in over 200 ms (no fade with reduced motion). The footer, the scrim, the name and the link are unchanged from the signed-off Past card (#705, #708).

Accessible names

  • Unchanged: each card is one link named Coast road loop, Jul 18–23, 5 nights, about 612 mi, Ada Brook and June Brook; the map image is decorative (alt="", inside the aria-hidden media box).

Tokens and components

  • RouteTile.svelte hosts an <img loading="lazy" referrerpolicy="no-referrer"> and an SVG overlay: the route in --map-stay (drives) and --map-route (flights), stops in --map-stay, a 2 px --map-sea ring. No new token.
  • Kinds tile: today’s bg-surface-3 with TripKinds top right; at 50% opacity while loading.

What Apple is sent

  • The card’s centre and zoom (no closer than city level, zoom 12) and its size, from the viewer’s browser, for cards within about a screen of view. Never the stops, the path, a name or a date.
  • The photo card and the one-stop card ask Apple nothing.
a2

No key, or the map fails

With no MapKit key on the server, or when a card’s snapshot address can’t be had or its image fails (Apple refuses, the network drops, the day’s allowance is spent), the card shows the kinds tile at full strength: the same tile it shows today for a trip it can’t place. No message, no retry until the page is reloaded, and no change of the card’s size, so the list never shifts.

Rule

  • No key: app.state.mapkit is false, so no card asks for an address or an image, and the page’s CSP names no Apple host.
  • Failure: onerror on the image, or the batch route answers without the trip; the tile stays.

Copy

  • None. The docs say maps need a MapKit key (frame c4).
a3

How many maps load, and the options that were weighed

On phone the list shows the newest year and loads the next near the bottom; on desktop every year. Either way only the cards within about a screen of the viewport ask for an image (the browser’s lazy loading), and the batch route signs the addresses of the cards on screen, at most 12 at a time. A household with 150 past trips, half with photos, that scrolls the whole list loads at most 75 images, once each a day.
