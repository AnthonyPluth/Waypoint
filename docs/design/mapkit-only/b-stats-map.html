MapKit only: Stats map

How the Stats world map looks and loads on MapKit, with the visited countries and states still shaded (decided on #833), at phone width (390 px) and desktop width (1280 px; the 960 px content column). Drawn with Waypoint’s tokens (frontend/src/app.css), dark only. b

The Stats map on one MapKit map, with the visited shading

One live MapKit map replaces TravelMap.svelte in #stats-map-slot (decided on #833: the shading stays). Apple’s dark style with points of interest off; on it, as MapKit overlays from the bottom up: the visited countries as mapkit.PolygonOverlays drawn from Waypoint’s bundled outlines (world-atlas 1:50m), filled in --map-visited at about 35% with an edge at about 70%; the flights as great-circle lines whose width follows the number of flights (--map-route); the airports as dots sized by visits (--map-airport); the stays as small pins (--map-stay). Fitted to the period’s places as today. MapKit’s own zoom control replaces Waypoint’s buttons.

How the shading is drawn

  • Only the visited countries and states become overlays (a household’s few, not the world). visitedFeatureIds and visitedStateTrips pick them as today; topojson-client turns them into rings; MapKit draws them with a mapkit.Style (fillColor, fillOpacity, strokeColor, lineWidth).
  • The outline files are the app’s own, loaded from this Waypoint when the map is shown; never from Apple, and not loaded at all with no key.
  • To confirm while building: overlay smoothness on a phone, overlay selection, and overlays sitting under the dots.

Interaction

  • Phone: one-finger drag scrolls the page until the map is zoomed in with + (then it pans), as today’s map pans only when zoomed in. Desktop: drag pans, the wheel zooms.
  • A tap on a country, state, airport, route or stay selects it and fills the caption under the map (b3).

What Apple learns

  • The tiles of the area on screen and of every area the viewer pans or zooms to.
  • Apple’s script is handed the period’s airports, arcs and stays and the visited countries’ and states’ outlines to draw, so it could read which ones the household visited. The outlines are bundled, never fetched from Apple.
b2

Zoomed in: the visited states

Once the map shows a span under about 40° of longitude, the visited US states appear as their own overlays from us-atlas (the same fill and edge), and the United States’ own country fill is dropped so the shading doesn’t double up. Unvisited states get no overlay; the faint state lines here are Apple’s own map. (The stand-in draws the states as a rough grid.)

b3

Tapped, and loading

A tap shows the place and each of its trips with dates under the map, as today, for a country or state as for an airport, route or stay; the selected shape or dot gets a white edge. While MapKit, the outlines and the first tiles load, the slot shows today’s skeleton (Loading the map, h-56, pulsing unless reduced motion). If only the outlines fail, the map shows without shading.

b4

No key, or the map fails

With no key the section keeps its heading, the tiles and the lists; there is no map slot and no skeleton, so nothing appears and then disappears, and the outline files are not loaded. If MapKit fails to load, refuses its token or errors later, the slot collapses to this.

Rule

  • The slot exists only while app.state.mapkit is true and the viewer is online; a failure removes it. No message.
