MapKit only: Replay with no key

The route replay and the completed trip page with and without a MapKit key, at phone width (390 px) and desktop width (1280 px). Drawn with Waypoint’s tokens (frontend/src/app.css), dark only. Apple’s map is a stylised stand-in (a real tile can’t be committed); the yellow tags are notes for the reader, not part of the app. Every name and place is made up. See the README for the inventory, privacy statement and build plan.

What this shows

  1. A completed trip’s page with and without a key on phone: the map hero (c1) and the photo hero with Replay trip (c2).
  2. The desktop left column with and without a key, and the replay failing after it started (c3).

c

A completed trip with fewer than three photos

With a key, the map hero is today’s (owner decision of 2026-11-16): the title and dates, the replay on Apple’s map with its controls, No photos yet, then the facts. With no key, or when MapKit fails before the replay shows, the map block isn’t there: the title and dates, No photos yet, the facts and the Bookings row. Nothing says why; the docs and the proposed Settings row (c4) do.

c2

A completed trip with photos

With a key the facts line offers Replay trip, which opens the replay inline under it (owner decision of 2026-11-16). With no key the button is not there.

c3

Desktop, and a failure during the replay

On desktop the 400 px left column holds the facts, the map with Replay trip and the bookings; with no key the map block and Replay trip are gone and the bookings move up. If MapKit errors after the replay started, the replay’s block closes with its controls and a short quiet line, The map couldn’t load, takes its place and is announced politely (decided on #833); a failure before the map appeared is silent. Switch to desktop to see c3’s two frames.

c4

Where it says maps need a key

The docs’ Trips and Stats pages open their map sections with Maps need a MapKit key on the server. Without one Waypoint shows no maps or route replays. And a read-only Maps row in Settings → Travel, linking to the docs (decided on #833).
