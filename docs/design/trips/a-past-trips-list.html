Past trips: photo-led cards

Past trips lead with the trip's photos: a mosaic (one large photo, two small) with the name on a scrim, the photo count in a pill, and a one-line footer with dates, nights and distance and the avatars of the people on the trip. Cards are grouped by year with the year's totals. One column on phone, two across on desktop (the 960 px content column). Upcoming keeps the countdown cards it has today.

Accessible names

  • Each card is one link, named Lisbon + Alentejo, Sep 3–10, 7 nights, 534 mi, 168 photos, Ada Brook and June Brook; the mosaic, the pill and the avatars inside it are decorative and hidden from the reader.
  • Each year is an h2 with its totals beside it (4 trips · 612 photos); each year's cards are a list named Past trips in 2025, as today.
  • Show 2023 is a button, as today: on phone only the newest year shows.

Tokens and components

  • Card: rounded-2xl border border-border bg-card shadow-card, the footer in text-sm text-muted-foreground with the dates in text-foreground font-semibold; AvatarStack as on the current card.
  • Name on a scrim over the large photo only (the scrim is a vertical gradient from clear to --background at 84%); the photo-count pill is rgb(7 9 14 / 72%) with white text.
  • No new token. Distance is in the household's unit (distance_unit), one unit throughout; the pill counts only photos the viewer may see.

Replaces

  • The year-grouped rows in frontend/src/pages/Trips.svelte (pastList: name and dates, kinds and avatars on a row) for Past trips only.
  • TripCard.svelte is untouched: Upcoming keeps its countdown cards (a1 and a2 show one for context), because a trip that has not happened has no photos yet.
  • A trip with no photos, or a viewer who may see none, is the open question about the fallback in the README.
