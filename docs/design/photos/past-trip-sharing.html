Notes

What stays the same and what the build would do once a placement is signed off.

Accessible names and keys

  • The trigger is a text button, Sharing: its visible text is its accessible name, with aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded and (on desktop) aria-controls, exactly as on a current trip.
  • Focus order in A: back, edit, options, the hero’s page content, View photos, Sharing, the facts line, Replay, then the days. In B: Photos by day then Sharing. In C: Replay then Sharing.
  • The panel is role="dialog" titled Photo sharing for this trip; Escape closes it and returns focus to the button. The popover is non-modal and closes on an outside click or Tab leaving it; the sheet is modal. Stop sharing on this trip opens the same confirmation on top.
  • The first-visit prompt and the reminder keep their own headings and buttons and never move focus.

What does not change

  • The panel’s words, radio group, Stop sharing on this trip and Removed from this trip (N) are #744’s; so are who sees a button (only the people the viewer acts for) and what each choice does.
  • No route, no photos.visible, no scope check changes: this moves a trigger and removes the inline card from the completed page.
  • Tokens and components are the app’s own: Button outline small, the popover and Sheet of #744. No new token.
  • The desktop frames use the built page: one column at most 48 rem wide (max-w-3xl), not the two-column page of trips b2.

Decisions of 2026-11-17

The owner answered the questions after seeing the frames, in chat on #787 and #772. Nothing is open; the build is the one in #772.

  1. Placement. A: Sharing sits beside View photos in the row under the hero. B (a visible Photos by day header) and C (in the facts line beside Replay) are not chosen; they stay on the page for the record.
  2. A page with fewer than three photos (map hero, no View photos): Sharing sits alone on its own row under the map, as drawn (S5).
  3. The reminder after Not now moves up and sits directly under the button row, no button (S3), instead of below the last day.
  4. The button’s space is held from the start. While the sharing state loads, the slot where Sharing will be is reserved, so nothing jumps when it arrives (S6; with the map hero a blank row is held under the map, S7). Once the state is known and the person has no control, the slot collapses: a person with no control sees a blank space only while it loads, then none. Beside View photos the collapse moves nothing; with the map hero the row under the map collapses and the page below moves up once. The owner accepted that one-time collapse on 2026-11-17.
  5. A left-aligned popover under the left-hand button is fine (A; B and C would be right-aligned, as on a current trip). Default, no objection raised.
  6. Select stays off the completed page. The panel is the only sharing control there; a photo is taken off from the viewer (#748). Default, no objection raised.
