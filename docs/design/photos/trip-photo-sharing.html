Notes

Accessible names and keys

  • Sharing: a text button; its visible text Sharing is its accessible name (no icon, no extra label), with aria-haspopup="dialog", aria-expanded and aria-controls.
  • Focus order on the page: Photos heading, Sharing, Select, See all, the strip.
  • Opened panel is role="dialog" titled Photo sharing for this trip. Focus moves to that title (as the app’s Sheet does); Tab goes: Close, then each person’s radio group (Who can see your photos on this trip: Only me, Everyone on this trip), Stop sharing on this trip, Removed from this trip (2) and its Put back buttons.
  • Escape closes; focus returns to the Sharing button. The sheet is modal (page behind inert). The desktop popover is non-modal: Escape or a click outside closes it, and Tab leaving it closes it and continues from the Sharing button.
  • Stop sharing on this trip still opens the existing alertdialog on top of the panel (Keep sharing focused first); closing it returns to the panel. Widening to Everyone still asks first, as today.
  • Each person the viewer acts for has their own named section (June’s photos on this trip); the viewer’s own comes first and the others start collapsed.

Who sees the Sharing button

  • Today’s code shows sharing controls only to the people the viewer acts for: the viewer’s own person, plus a managed person without a sign-in who has a photo grant (actingPersons in trip-sharing.svelte.ts). Other travellers on the trip never see any sharing control, only the gallery they may see.
  • The button shows only while there is a sharing card to manage. The first-visit prompt and the reminder row after Not now stay inline with no button. People who cannot change anything see no button, not a disabled one.
  • Nothing about who sees what changes: the button only opens the same controls, and every request, grant and scope check is the one that exists today.
