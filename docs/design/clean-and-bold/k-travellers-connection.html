Several travellers and a connection on the hero

Open for the owner

Recommended. Travellers: the Seats fact shows the first seat and “+N” when there are more than two, and a row under the facts lists each traveller with their seat (neutral avatars, the seat in Geist Mono), shown only where the app shows travellers today and only when there are two or more. Connection: the codes are the whole journey (BOS to LIS) with the stop as a dot on the arc and “1 stop, JFK” under it; the big fact, the facts and the seats are the leg you are on; a “Then” line under a hairline names the next leg (its number, time, terminal and the time to connect). During the connection the plane waits at the stop, the travelled part is blue, and the line becomes “Earlier”.

Colour

  • Blue: the countdown, the plane and the travelled arc.
  • Green: “on time” for the leg you are on.
  • A late next leg turns its time in the “Then” line amber, and a connection under 45 minutes says so in amber (“38 min to connect”).

Open for the owner

  • Whole journey codes (drawn) or the current leg's codes with the stop named.
  • The per-traveller row on the hero, or seats only in the opened booking.
  • “14A +2”, or “3 seats”.
  • The 45-minute short-connection rule is a suggestion; the app has no such rule today.
