s1

Stats with MapKit on

Open for the owner

The page for one person and one year, from the top to Flights: the summary label, the title, the filters (a row with the Filters button on phone, the year chips and travellers on desktop), “See your 2025 in review”, the headline numbers, Where you’ve been with the map slot, and Flights. The map is drawn by Apple's MapKit in the browser, so this file draws only its slot.

Kept from today

  • Every section, tile, list and record, their labels and order; lists show five with “Show all N”; tiles appear only when there is something to count.
  • The filters: Filters with its blue dot when in use and the sheet (s4) on phone, the year chips and travellers on one bar on desktop; the summary line as a polite status.
  • “See your 2025 in review” only for a past year, or the current year in December.
  • Distance and its comparisons (“1.3× around the Earth”), the units the server gives.

What changes

  • Tiles are black cards with 24 px corners; the headline tiles lose today's blue border and are bigger instead.
  • Section icons are the kind outlines of #876 (flight, the bed for a stay, car, parking, cruise), no tile behind them.
  • Numbers in tiles keep Geist Mono as today; the headline numbers are heavy Inter like the hero's big fact.

Open for the owner

  • The seat bars in white on the hairline track (drawn), one colour, since #876 colours only blue, green, amber and the kinds; or today's four chart colours.
  • The headline tiles: kept as the page's top row (drawn; Upcoming has no stat tiles, but Stats is the page for them) or folded into the summary line.
s2

The map slot: no key, offline and loading

With no MapKit key on the server, offline, with nothing to place or after the map failed, the page has no map slot at all: the Countries list follows the tiles. While MapKit loads, the slot is a quiet block. This is today's behaviour, unchanged; the map itself stays Apple's (signed off in docs/design/mapkit-only/).

Kept from today

  • No bundled map, no placeholder, no message when there is no key: the page simply has no map, as built in #835 to #839.

What changes

  • The loading block is the dark surface with 24 px corners.

s5

Your year in review

Open for the owner

The full-screen review a past year offers: one card at a time with Back and Next, the progress at the top and Close. Its cards and words are today's; the last card makes the image on the device and saves it.

Kept from today

  • The cards and their order (distance, flights, countries, top route, nights, cruises, parking, the map, Save as image), each shown only when it has something; the arrow keys and Escape; “The country outlines couldn’t load…”.
  • The map card and the saved image are drawn on the device from the year's numbers (mapSvg, cardSvg in frontend/src/lib/review.ts), so they are slots here, not designs.

What changes

  • The page is the page colour; the progress segments are blue up to the current card; Next and Save as image are the one blue action of each card; the card label is grey.

Open for the owner

  • The card label grey (drawn) instead of today's amber signal ink, since amber means needs attention.
  • The saved image and the map card keep their own look as built (drawn as slots), or a follow-up redraws them in the new look; they are images people share, so the change would show outside Waypoint.
