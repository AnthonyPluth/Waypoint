The completed-trip page

Open for the owner

Replaces the look of frontend/src/lib/components/PastTrip.svelte (#709, phone; #710, desktop) and what it holds: the back link, Edit trip details and Trip options, the hero (photos, or the map when fewer than three photos can be shown), the Sharing control and the reminder, the facts line, the total, the need-a-look link, Replay, the notes, the Bookings row (phone) or list (desktop), the photos by day, Show all N days, Open gallery and the “Done” card. Nothing is added or taken away; the map is the route alone, as the owner decided on 2026-11-18.

What changes

  • Cards and the hero are black or photo with 24 px corners and no border or shadow; the hero's scrim ends in black.
  • The facts line: the first fact white and heavy, the rest grey, one size larger than today (15 px).
  • “3 photos need a look” is a quiet line with an amber dot, like the review line on Upcoming (today a soft amber chip).
  • The day rail's dot is label grey, not blue: a past day is not live (open for the owner).
  • Far stays an amber badge on its photo (it needs a look); the video and itinerary marks stay as today.

What of #705 stays

  • All of it, with the owner decisions recorded in docs/design/trips/README.md: the mosaic hero (three on phone, seven on desktop) with each photo once, the facts line and its desktop form with the place count, the Bookings row and sheet, the 400 px column that stays in view, six photos a day on phone and up to twelve on desktop, Show all N days, Open gallery, Replay in the column, Edit trip details by the pencil, the notes under the facts.
  • The removals of 2026-11-18 stand: no photo pins, no stops list, no day glyph.

Photos are shared by their person

  • The hero, the day grids, the counts (“168 photos”, “12 photos”), the need-a-look count and the map are built only from photos the viewer may see: p3 shows a viewer whose own 24 photos are kept to themselves, so the trip shows 144, and only they see the reminder.
  • Sharing is per person and only for the people you act for; the sheet and the popover are today's TripPhotoSharingMenu restyled. Nobody can tell another person's unshared photos exist.
  • Made-up trip, people and amounts; the photos are vector stand-ins.

Kept exactly as today

  • Every word: “Past trips”, “Sharing”, “Share…”, “Replay”, “Replay trip”, “No photos yet”, “Photos by day”, “Open gallery”, “Show all 8 days”, “Bookings · 7”, the Done card's text and button, the Trip options items.
  • Total only when costs are shown; Replay only with two stops or more and not for reduced motion on desktop.
  • No colour is the page's one blue action here: nothing on a finished trip is that urgent (open for the owner).
