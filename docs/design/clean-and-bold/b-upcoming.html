Reconstructed, not the original canvas

#876 asked for the owner's approved mockups to be put here, but they live only on the owner's private design canvas, which the agents working on this repository cannot open. This file is a reconstruction: drawn from the decisions written in #876 and #875, the tokens of #880, and the components as built in #899, #902, #904, #905 and #906, so it shows what the app now shows. Where the build departs from a written decision it is drawn as built and listed in the README. Every name, place, flight, seat, code, price and date is made up.

What this shows

  1. The next-up flight hero with its footer, one quiet review line, the day-by-day list and Later, at phone width.
  2. The same at desktop width, hero and day list in two columns.

Upcoming, phone and desktop

Upcoming as Upcoming.svelte draws it since #905 and #907, when the next booking is a flight: the date over the 40 px or 64 px title, the offline status and Add a booking; one quiet line for emails to review; the next flight as the hero, under “Next up” and the trip's name; then the trip day by day with the blue Next marker on the first day still to come; then Later. No stat tiles and no banners. On desktop the hero and the day list (“Coming up”) sit in two columns from a 900 px content width. When the next booking is not a flight, today's pass card stays (point 11 on #878) and the list goes under it; that case is in the make verify images, not drawn here.

Hero footer, as built

  • The flight hero on Upcoming keeps the footer it had before #905: the airline action (“Check in” in the 24 hours before, otherwise “Open in app” or “Manage booking”), the confirmation code with Copy, Open trip, the travellers and the cost. #876 decision 5 puts these behind a tap; see the README's departures.
  • The hero has no “Confirmation, travellers and price” row here (it links to the live flight view instead).

Day list

  • Time in Geist Mono, the brand logo or the outline kind icon, the title and a grey line, a chevron; a booking with a ready QR code shows a blue “Show code” in place of the chevron (frame i).
  • Times are where they happen and carry no zone in the list.

Colour

  • Blue: countdown, plane, the Next marker.
  • Green: “on time”; the saved-for-offline status.
  • Amber: the review dot and badge; the Later dates (owner decision, #907).
  • The “Check in” button is the action blue #3563e0 of the existing --action token, not the status blue.
