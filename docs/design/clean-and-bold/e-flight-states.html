Flight hero states, facts and zones

Item 1, 2 and 3 of #878: the flight hero card in the four states the clean and bold mockups (#876) do not draw, which three facts each of the eight states shows, and where a time's zone and a delayed flight's original time sit. The look is the tokens of #880. Phone frames are the 358 px card on a 390 px screen; the desktop toggle shows the card at the 576 px width of the desktop trip page's "Next up" column, with the desktop type sizes. Every flight, seat and time here is made up.

1a

Boarding

Boarding is derived today (a live status with a gate, within 40 minutes of departure). The card reads like the on-time card of #876, with the top-right marker saying Boarding. The gate is a fact, not the big fact, so the card does not repeat itself (owner rule from #714).

Colour

  • Blue: the "Boarding" marker with a live dot, and the plane at the origin.
  • Green: "on time" in the quiet line.
  • No amber.

Facts

  • Terminal, Gate, Seats (departure side).
  • Big fact: Departs and the time. Sentence: Boarding now, to the city.
1b

Cancelled

Open for the owner

Today the pill is red (the bad tone); #876 allows only blue, green and amber, so the recommendation is the amber pill: it needs attention. The codes and times go grey and the times are struck through; the arc is dotted grey with no plane, as today (no plane on a cancelled flight).

Colour

  • Amber: the "Cancelled" pill only.
  • No blue (nothing live, no plane) and no green.
  • Open for the owner: amber, or a white-on-grey pill so amber stays for changes you can still act on.

Facts

  • Terminal and Gate are a dash (live values are not shown for a cancelled flight today).
  • Seats stay: they come from the booking.
  • Quiet line: today's sentence, “Contact Aurora Air about rebooking.”
1c

Diverted

The destination code becomes the airport it is diverted to, with the booked one struck through small above it. The plane keeps its place on the arc while the flight is in the air. Arrival facts are dashes: today the card shows no live arrival values once a flight is diverted.

Colour

  • Amber: the "Diverted" pill and the new arrival time.
  • Blue: the plane and the travelled part of the arc (it is live).
  • No green.

Facts

  • Arrival term, Arrival gate, Bag claim, all a dash.
  • Quiet line: “Was headed to Lisbon” (or “Where it lands isn’t known yet.” with no other airport) and the update time.
1d

Scheduled, no live data

More than a day before departure there is no live status. The card is the on-time card without the green “on time” (nothing says it is on time yet); the quiet line says the times are from the booking, which replaces the booked marks of #666 for the whole card.

Colour

  • Blue: the countdown “In 12 days” and the plane at the origin.
  • No green and no amber.

Facts

  • Terminal from the booking when it has one, Gate a dash, Seats from the booking.
  • Quiet line: the date, then “Times from your booking. Live status starts 1 day before.”

The three facts in every state, and what replaces the fourth fact and the marks

Before departure the facts are the departure side, from take-off on the arrival side. An unknown value is a dash in grey, never a missing column. The fourth fact of #666 (Cabin before departure, Seat on arrival) leaves the hero and stays in the opened booking (frame g), which already lists Seat and Cabin. Belt is renamed Bag claim, as #881 words it. The live dot and booked ring of #666 go: where values come from is said once for the whole card in the quiet line (“Updated 9 minutes ago” for live status, “Times from your booking” with none). A late landing (“12 min late”) is amber, under the rule that amber is a change.

3

Where a time's zone shows, and the struck-through original time

Open for the owner

Recommended: each time under an airport code carries its zone as a small grey abbreviation on its own line (EST, WET), so each time reads as the clock at that airport and nothing is converted. The big fact and the sentences carry no zone: they name the place (“Departs 6:55 AM” is under BOS). When delayed, the booked time sits struck through and small above the new one, and the quiet line repeats it once (“45 min late · was 6:10 AM”). An alternative is a zone only when the two airports' zones differ; that hides the zone on most domestic flights, which is why it is not the recommendation.

Zone

  • Under each time, 11 px Geist Mono, the label grey #8f8e98.
  • The abbreviation the app already has for that airport's zone at that date; with none, the zone is left out rather than guessed.
  • Lists and the day-by-day list keep the time alone (they name the place).

Delayed time

  • Struck time 12 px grey above the new time; the new time amber.
  • Both airports' times move: the arrival time is the new estimate when live status gives one.
  • Quiet line: “N min late · was ” with the old time struck through.
