Reconstructed, not the original canvas

#876 asked for the owner's approved mockups to be put here, but they live only on the owner's private design canvas, which the agents working on this repository cannot open. This file is a reconstruction: drawn from the decisions written in #876 and #875, the tokens of #880, and the components as built in #899, #902, #904, #905 and #906. Where the build departs from a written decision it is drawn as built and listed in the README, except four points the owner decided on 2026-11-21 (Geist Mono codes on the hero, no edge or shadow on the hero, the code, travellers and cost behind a tap on Upcoming, a bed for a stay): those are drawn as decided, and the app changes to match in follow-up pull requests. Every name, place, flight, seat, code, price and date is made up.

What this shows

  1. The flight hero card in its four drawn states.
  2. The stay, car and train cards, and the compact card of a flight that is not the hero.
c1–4

The flight hero card in four states

FlightHero.svelte (#902) for on time, delayed, in the air and landed: the carrier line with one marker at the top right, a plain sentence over one big fact, the two airport codes on a dotted arc with the plane, each time with its zone under it, the hairline, then three label-over-value facts. The other four states (boarding, cancelled, diverted, no live data) are in frame e. The card's type grows from a card width of 512 px: the desktop toggle shows it in a 576 px column.

Type

  • Airport codes 36 px (44 px wide) in Geist Mono, weight 700. Owner decision 2026-11-21 (#876): the app changes to match, in follow-up pull requests. (The build of #902 sets them in Inter.)
  • Times in Geist Mono: 15 px under the codes (17 px wide) and in the big fact, 34 px (44 px wide).
  • Facts 22 px (26 px wide), weight 700; labels 11 px, weight 600, uppercase, 0.09em, #8f8e98.

Edge and amber

  • No edge and no shadow, like the booking cards (decision 1). Owner decision 2026-11-21 (#876): the app changes to match, in follow-up pull requests. (The build of #902 still draws a 1 px edge and the card shadow.)
  • A late time is drawn in the light amber ink #ffd58a (--signal-ink) for contrast; the pill is #eba43b.
c5–8

Stay, car and train cards

BookingCard.svelte (#904): a black 24 px card, the kind icon as a coloured outline with no chip, the name, a grey line ending in the status, one hairline and three facts. A stay keeps its brand logo at the right (owner's wish, #904). A tap opens the booking (frame g); a booking with a ready QR code adds a blue “Show code” button (frame f). The compact flight card is drawn too, because the trip page uses it for every flight that is not the hero.

Facts

  • Stay: Check-in, Nights, Check-out (dates; the times are in the opened booking).
  • Car: Pick-up, Days, Return. Train: Departs, From (the origin station), Arrives.
  • Flight (not the hero): Departs, Flight, Arrives; an arrival on another day carries “+1 day”.

Colour

  • Icon strokes: stay #38c6d9, car #f4d35e, train #c79bff, flight #4f9dff.
  • Green “Confirmed”; “Changed” and “Cancelled” in amber (frame f).
  • The stay icon is a bed. Owner decision 2026-11-21 (#876): the app changes to match, in follow-up pull requests. (The app still draws a building.)
