Buttons

Open for the owner

ui/button’s seven variants become five looks. Default (the primary) is blue, and only for the one primary action of a screen or section; outline and secondary become one plain button on the fill grey; ghost stays quiet; link is white and underlined; destructive is the plain button with an amber label. The action variant (--action, #3563e0) is drawn as the blue primary. Corners are 14 px (12 px small), heights 44 and 36 px, with the 44 px tap area on the phone kept. Labels are today’s.

Open for the owner

  • Destructive actions: plain with an amber label (drawn, also for groups A and B) or today’s red --destructive #ff7b72. #876 allows only blue, green and amber; red would be a fourth colour.
  • The action variant folds into the blue primary (drawn) or keeps its own deeper blue.
  • Blue Save in each Settings section (drawn: each section is its own task) or one blue per page.

Fills

  • Buttons use the fill grey #1a1920 and inputs and menus the well #141319 that frames e to k introduced. The shipped --secondary (#1a1e29), --input (#2c3243) and --popover (#181c26) are still the earlier blue-greys; this design does not change them (the build issue would, after the sign-off).

Forms and inputs

Open for the owner

ui/input, the native select, textarea, date, file and search fields, and the checkbox, radio and switch rows of Settings and People. A label in white above, the field on the well grey with 14 px corners and no border, help in grey under it. Focus is the 2 px blue ring (--ring); a problem is an amber ring with the message under the field in amber (today a tinted amber box). Chosen is white: a ticked box is white with a black tick, a chosen radio a white ring, a switch that is on a white track with a black knob. Field text is 16 px on the phone, so iOS does not zoom.

Open for the owner

  • Switches and ticks in white (drawn) or blue.
  • Form errors as an amber line under the field (drawn) or today’s tinted amber box.
s3

Status chips

Open for the owner

StatusChip and Badge. A status becomes a coloured dot and word with no tinted background, as #876 draws “Confirmed” and “on time”; the amber filled pill is kept for Delayed only, as on the hero. The tones follow the colour rules: green ok, amber for warn and for bad (cancelled is “you may need to act”, #878 open point 1), blue for live (departed, syncing), grey for quiet. Badges that are not a status (Member, Guest, Local, an assistant’s access) become grey tags. When a chip’s word changes it keeps today’s short fade.

Dialogs

ConfirmDialog: a black card with 30 px corners over a 62% black scrim, centred, 460 px wide from md. Title, today’s description, then Cancel and the confirm, right-aligned; Cancel takes focus. A confirm that is not destructive is the blue action; a destructive one is plain with an amber label; type-to-confirm keeps today’s field and leaves the confirm disabled until the word matches; the alert form (Resume a library) keeps its own cancel word (“Not now”). Busy words are today’s (“Removing…”).

Sheets

Sheet: on the phone a black bottom sheet with 30 px top corners, the grab handle (drag down to close), the title in 20 px heavy type with its description under it, an optional lead and actions, and Close. On desktop the same sheet is the 544 px side panel of g-booking-detail.html. A sheet with a guard asks “Discard changes?” (Keep editing, Discard changes) before it closes on unsaved typing, as today.

Kept

  • Every sheet’s title, description and focus order, the drag to close, and the guard.
  • Where a page opens a form in place on desktop (People, Settings), it stays in place.

Open for the owner

s8

Map surfaces: the MapKit slot and its no-key state

Open for the owner

Every map is Apple’s MapKit, as signed off in mapkit-only (#828) and built in #835 to #839; nothing here changes when or what Apple is asked. In the new look the slot is a rectangle with 24 px corners on the map’s sea colour, no border and no shadow (today a hairline border and elevation 1), Apple’s controls on a black chip, and the attribution left as Apple draws it. Loading is a black placeholder card. A replay that fails says “The map couldn’t load” in a hairline box, as today. With no key, offline or with nothing to place: a Trips card shows the kinds tile (outline icons on the well grey), and the Stats map and the replay leave no slot at all. The map’s own colours are the shipped --map-* tokens.

Open for the owner

  • The map overlays keep the shipped --map-airport (#f6b73c), --map-stay (#4fd1b0) and --map-visited (#3d7bc7) (drawn), or move to the kind and status colours of #876 (airport amber is close to “needs attention”).
  • The slot with no border or shadow (drawn) or today’s hairline border.
