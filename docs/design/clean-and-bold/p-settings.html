Mail & review: Gmail and AI

Open for the owner

Each connected mailbox is a black card with today’s contents: the address and its state (Connected in green, Reconnect or “Couldn’t reach Google” in amber), “Waypoint can read this mailbox, read-only.”, the last scan and its summary (or “Scanning for bookings…” in blue while it runs), the household sharing checkbox, the labels line with Choose labels and its list, Ignored senders, Review at a glance, any scan notice or error, and Scan now, Look back through all mail and Disconnect. Under them “Add another Gmail” (or “Connect your Gmail”, or “Gmail isn’t set up” with the two settings and How). Then AI: the sentence, “How email is shared”, and the Ollama address and Model fields. Waypoint has no hosted AI provider; the only AI is the household’s own Ollama server, and nothing else is drawn. Addresses, senders and counts are made up.

Privacy, as built

  • Review at a glance shows counts and sender domains only, never anything from a message.
  • Mailboxes are each member’s own, as the page says: “Only you see what you connect.”
  • The AI section offers an Ollama address only.

Open for the owner

  • Connected as a green status word (drawn) instead of today’s outline badge.
  • Disconnect and Ignore with an amber label, not red (see p-shared s2).
s4

Photos: libraries and photo sharing

Photos sources as built: each Immich library with its person, address, what happened at the last sync and its state (Up to date green; Syncing blue; Not up to date, Can’t reach Immich, Key refused and Key lost amber; Paused, Not synced yet and Connected grey), and for your own or a managed library Sync now, Change key, Pause or Resume, and Remove. Needs a look lists the photos Waypoint wasn’t sure of, only to their person (or whoever manages the library), with Keep on this trip, Pick a trip, Remove from this trip or Leave off. Connect an Immich library opens the form: whose library, address, API key with Show, the permissions to tick, what Waypoint reads, Test and Save. Photo sharing then has the person switch, the default scope, Share on all my trips, and the trips with their photos, each shared (with its scope and Stop sharing) or Not shared (with Share…). Thumbnails are drawn as gradient stand-ins.

Privacy, as built

  • Photos are shared by their person: Needs a look and the trip list show only the libraries the viewer owns or manages.
  • The API key field is drawn masked; the key “is stored encrypted and is never shown again”.
