Open for the owner

Owner decision, 2026-11-20: this is BookingDetail.svelte (#712) restyled, one component for completed and upcoming trips. It keeps its placement (a bottom sheet on phone, a right panel up to 34 rem on desktop), its header (kind icon, name, subtitle, an always-visible Edit, More with Move and Remove, Close) and its rows, which come from Segment.details as today. What a trip not yet over adds is the action row the old booking card had: email, directions, call and the airline app, each shown only when the booking has it, as today. A completed trip shows no action row (g3), as today.

What is new in the restyle

  • The sheet and panel are true black on the dimmed page, 30 px corners on the sheet's top.
  • Labels 11 px uppercase grey over a hairline per row; values white, 15 px.
  • The confirmation code in Geist Mono with Copy, as a 44 px button.
  • The status in the subtitle follows the colour rules: green Confirmed, amber Changed or Cancelled.
  • The action row: up to three tiles across, a second row when a booking has more; none is blue (the one blue action on a trip page is “Show code”).
