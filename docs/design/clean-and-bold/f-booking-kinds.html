Booking cards: cruise, parking, visit, changed and cancelled

Items 4 and 5 of #878: booking cards for a cruise, a parking booking and a past visit, and how a changed or cancelled booking reads when green means confirmed and amber means changed. Cards follow #876's stay, car and train cards: a black card with 24 px corners, a header row that is the tap target, a hairline, three facts, and “Show code” only on a booking with a QR code. Phone frames show cards on a 390 px screen; desktop shows them at the 576 px width of the “Then” column.

Cruise, parking and past visit

Open for the owner

Labels are the words the app uses today (START_WORD and END_WORD in frontend/src/lib/trips.ts: Embarks and Disembarks, Entry and Exit, From and To) with Nights or Days in the middle, as the owner adopted for #876's cards on 2026-11-20. A past visit has no status, so its grey line has no “Confirmed”. Parking is the one kind here that can have a QR code, so it carries “Show code”.

Icon colour (recommended)

  • Cruise #ff8fab, as #876 and #880 give it.
  • Parking #d9d8e0, a light neutral: today’s --kind-parking (#9be15d, lime) sits next to the green “Confirmed” the way the old stay teal did, the reason #880 moved stay and car.
  • Past visit #8f8e98, the label grey: today’s --kind-quiet is the muted grey, and a visit is a record, not a booking to act on.
  • All three are at least 3:1 on the black card.

Facts

  • Cruise: Embarks, Nights, Disembarks.
  • Parking: Entry (the time, in Geist Mono), Days, Exit.
  • Past visit: From, Days, To.
  • Open for the owner: parking and visit colours, and whether a single-day visit keeps From and To or shows only the day.
5

Changed and cancelled bookings

Open for the owner

Recommended: the word at the end of the grey line carries the status, in place of the green “Confirmed”: amber “Changed”, amber “Cancelled”. A changed booking also marks in amber the facts a newer email changed, when the app knows which (the “Updated from a newer email” note that BookingDetail.svelte shows names them); otherwise only the word is amber. A cancelled booking keeps its place in date order with its name in grey, its icon faded and its facts struck through, and loses “Show code”. No red anywhere, as #876 allows only blue, green and amber.

Colour

  • Amber: “Changed” and the changed values; “Cancelled”.
  • Green is never shown on a changed or cancelled card.
  • Open for the owner: cancelled in amber (recommended: you may need to rebook) or in grey (it no longer asks anything of you).

What the reader hears

  • The header row's accessible name ends with the status: “Oeste Car Hire, car, Thursday 11 March, cancelled”.
  • Struck-through values are read with “was”, as the cancelled stay of the completed-trip detail is today.
