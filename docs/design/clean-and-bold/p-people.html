p1

People: the list

Today’s page in the new look. The title and “Add a guest”, the page’s one blue action. The quiet line about who sees saved numbers. One black card per person in today’s order, with the name, a Member or Guest tag (no colour: it is a kind of person, not a status), Edit and, for a guest, Remove as 44 px icon buttons. Under the name: the legal name and how airlines print it, “Linked from guest …”, and the amber “Two numbers for …” line when a person has two numbers for one program. Then the numbers grouped by kind (Airline, Hotel, Car rental, Cruise, Known Traveler, Redress), each with its brand mark (a monogram where there is no logo), the program, the masked number as a tap target, its expiry and notes, and Edit and Remove. A number that can’t be decrypted says “Can’t be read with this key: enter it again” in place of the mask. At the foot, “Add loyalty or traveler number” and, on a guest when you aren’t linked to one, “This is me”. More reaches People on the phone (j3); the side navigation has it on desktop.

Privacy, as built

  • Every number is drawn masked: four dots and the last four digits, as mask() in domain/loyalty.py sends it. The mask digits here are placeholders (1234, 0000, 5678…), not numbers.
  • The full number only reaches the browser when someone taps it (POST /api/loyalty/{id}/reveal); it is never on the page by default and never kept in the browser’s storage.
  • Every signed-in member sees the same list, as today: “IDs are for the household.”

Colour

  • Blue: “Add a guest” only.
  • Amber: the two-numbers line (needs attention).
  • Nothing else is coloured: Member and Guest are grey tags, brand marks are monograms on the fill grey.
p2

Add a guest, edit a person

On the phone the form opens in the bottom sheet (Sheet, titled “Add a guest” or “Edit Ines Quill”); on desktop it opens in place above the list, titled, and “Add a guest” hides while it is open, as today. The fields are today’s: Display name (“How Waypoint shows them.”), First name, Legal name (“The name on their ID and bookings, used to match them.”), and Advanced matching with Name aliases (“How airlines print the name, one per line.”), open when the person has aliases. A household member’s form adds “They’re a household member: their link to their sign-in stays as it is.” Save (or Add guest) is the blue action; Cancel is plain and asks “Discard changes?” when something was typed (p-shared s5).

Kept

  • Every field, limit and help text of today’s form, and the discard guard.
  • Inputs, the disclosure and the sheet are the shared pieces of p-shared.html.
p3

Add or edit a loyalty or traveler number

Today’s form: Kind (Airline, Hotel, Car rental, Cruise, Known Traveler, Redress), Program (the app’s list for the kind, without programs the person already holds, and Other with “Say which program in the notes.”), Number, the sentence on who can see it, Expiry for Known Traveler and Redress only, and Notes. When editing, the number field is empty with the placeholder “Leave empty to keep ••••5678”: the saved number is never put back into the field. On the phone it is a sheet; on desktop it opens in a nested well under the person’s numbers. Program names here are made up; the picker lists the app’s programs.

Privacy

  • The Number field is drawn empty or with its masked placeholder only.
  • The sentence “Every signed-in household member can see this number once they ask to show it. It’s encrypted and stays hidden until someone asks.” stays under the field, as today.
p4

Showing a number

Open for the owner

Tapping a masked number asks the server for it, shows it in place and copies it, with the toast “Copied” (or “Couldn’t copy it: select the number instead.” when the browser refuses). Tapping it again hides it; loading the page again hides every number. The shown state is the same tap target with a blue ring (it is live, now); the number itself is drawn as a hatched bar, because a design file never carries a number, real or real-looking.

Open for the owner

  • The ring on a shown number: blue (drawn, “now”) or no ring.
  • Whether the masked number keeps the look of a quiet button (drawn, so it reads as tappable) or goes back to plain text that underlines on hover, as built.

Confirmations

Open for the owner

Today’s three confirmations with their titles, texts and buttons, in the shared dialog (p-shared s4). Removing is destructive: its button is drawn as a plain button with an amber label, not red, since #876 allows only blue, green and amber; linking a guest is not destructive in that sense, so its confirm is the blue action, as today. Cancel is first and takes focus.

Open for the owner

  • Destructive confirms: plain with an amber label (drawn, shared with Settings and with groups A and B) or today’s red (--destructive, #ff7b72).

Loading, error and empty

Loading is one black placeholder card; a failed load is the shared alert with the error and Try again; with nobody yet, today’s sentence under the title and “Add a guest”. The error text is what the app says when the server can’t be reached.
