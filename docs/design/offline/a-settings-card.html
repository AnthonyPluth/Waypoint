The Offline access card in the iPhone app

Inside the iPhone app, saving trips is native: no passkey, no Face ID, and the copy lives in the app’s private storage. This is the Settings card that shows and switches saving, and every state it can be in. Phone width only, dark only, vector only. The decisions are in the last sections and in the README.

In place: Settings, Offline & notifications

The card keeps the app’s existing rows pattern and sits in the same place, first in the Offline & notifications group, above the reminders. Inside the iPhone app it replaces both the passkey card and the “isn’t available” card. Shown in its most common state (saved, two trips). The states follow in A to G.

A

Off

Saving is on by default, so this is the state of a person who switched it off. Switching off deletes the saved trips at once, with no confirmation, as in the browser. There is no setup sheet, no prompt and no device check; the switch is the whole control.

B

Saving

While the app is fetching the trips (the bridge status says it is saving now) the switch is disabled and the line says Saving your trips…. The card then becomes C, D or E.

C

Saved, with the trips and when

The saved trips are named with the time of the last save (“12 min ago”, the browser card’s wording), and a line says when each is removed. For many trips the list shows three names, then “and 7 more” with Show all and Show fewer. There is no Remove button: switching saving off is the only way to delete the saved trips.

D

On, nothing saved yet

The first state of a new install: saving is on but the first save happens at the first online open after signing in. The same words cover a phone with no upcoming trip, and one whose saved trips all ended more than 3 days ago and were removed.

E

Problems

A failed save keeps the older copy (the app never replaces a good copy with nothing) and is tried again at the next online open. A copy that can’t be read is deleted by switching saving off, then on. Both use the bridge’s new status flags (see the README).

F

Signed out

Settings can’t be opened while signed out, so there is no card. Signing out deletes the saved trips, and the app’s sign-out screen says so. After signing in again, saving is on.

G

Not supported

In a browser, including Safari on an iPhone, the card is exactly as today and its wording is not touched (shown for reference). An installed app too old to have the offline bridge shows no Offline access card at all.

H

Upcoming and the trip page

These panels read the same state as the card and keep their layout; only the wording changes inside the app (“this device”, no Face ID, no passkey). The green Saved for offline pill is unchanged. Shown in the order saved, trip not saved, off, nothing yet, can’t be read.

Offline saving is off on this device. Turn it on in Settings.

Nothing is saved yet. Your trips under way or starting within a week, and your next upcoming trip, are saved when you open Waypoint online.

The saved trips can’t be read. Switch saving off and on in Settings to start afresh.

Decisions, signed off 2026-11-17

The owner’s answers to the open questions. Nothing is left open.

  1. No Face ID: the phone’s lock is the gate. No encryption of Waypoint’s own; the files may go into iCloud and computer backups.
  2. No Remove button. Switching saving off is the only way to delete the saved trips, and it deletes them at once.
  3. The card says “this device”, not “this iPhone”.
  4. The card keeps the line that the saved copy can be part of iCloud and computer backups.
  5. No prompt to offer saving. Saving is on by default for a new install and can be switched off in Settings; the very first save happens at the first online open after signing in.
  6. The bridge’s status reply gains two flags, “saving now” and “last save failed”, so states B and E are real (shape in the README).
  7. The signed-out screen says “Saved trips were removed from this device.”
  8. After signing in again, saving is on.
  9. An app too old for the bridge shows no Offline access card. Real browsers keep today’s card unchanged.
  10. Phone width is the only width that ships.
  11. Ten saved trips show three names, then “and 7 more” with Show all.
  12. The card carries no sentence about how the trips are stored; that explanation lives in the docs. The backups line stays. Signed off with this change on 2026-11-17.

Notes

Accessible names and what is reused.

Accessible names

  • Section: Offline access (heading).
  • Status line: On for this device, Off on this device, Couldn’t save the trips, The saved trips can’t be read. The chip repeats the state in words, never by colour alone.
  • Switch: role="switch", name Save my upcoming trips, 44 px touch target. Disabled while saving.
  • Saving line is aria-live="polite"; the result (saved or failed) is announced there.

Tokens and components

  • Only existing tokens: --card, --border, --primary, --status-ok-*, --status-bad-*, --muted-foreground. No new token.
  • Components: the app’s rows and row, the existing switch input and the status chip as in the other Settings cards. No dialog and no button on the card.
  • Icons are the app’s lucide set: hard-drive-download, loader-circle.

What does not change

  • In a browser, the card, the passkey sheet and every word are as today.
  • No Face ID, no passkey, no device check anywhere in the app’s card.
  • Reminders and the calendar subscription below the card.
  • Every name, trip and time in these frames is made up.
