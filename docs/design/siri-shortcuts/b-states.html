Siri answers: when they can't answer

The same intents (write-up, #820) when the phone is locked, no one is signed in, saving is off, nothing is saved, nothing is found, or the copy is old. None of these answers holds any trip data, except the old copy, which is the normal answer with its age.

Signed off by the owner on 2026-11-18 (#820). Built in #840 to #843; the phrases and the defaults listed as open in the write-up may still change there.

F

Phone locked

Every intent asks for an unlock first (authenticationPolicy = .requiresAuthentication); Siri shows the system's prompt and Waypoint says nothing until the phone is unlocked. The saved trips can't be read while it is locked anyway (.complete file protection).

Notes

  • F1's wording is Apple's, not ours; confirm on a device.
  • F2 is Waypoint's fallback if a read fails at the moment of locking. No trip, no hotel, no time.
  • With AirPods or CarPlay and the phone locked: F1. A HomePod gets nothing.
G

Nothing to answer from

Short spoken answers with no card. Each says what to do, in the app.

Rules

  • G1: no server in the session, or no saved copy for it (sign-out deletes the copy).
  • G2: offlineSaving is off.
  • G3: saving is on and nothing is saved.
  • G4: a copy whose version or shape the app doesn't know.
  • None of them calls the server.
H

Nothing found, and an old copy

When the saved trips hold no flight or stay, the answer says saved on this iPhone, because a trip further out isn't saved. An old copy answers as usual and says its age on the card.

Old copy

  • After a day without an online open, the chip turns to Old copy and the footer says to open Waypoint.
  • Whether the spoken answer says so too (as of Monday) is still open; the default is the card only.
  • H4: a copy saved before the server sent city names speaks the flight number instead, until the next online open replaces it.
