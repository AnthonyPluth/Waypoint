Siri answers: next flight and tonight's stay

Mock frames for the two intents the write-up recommends building first (#820). Phone width only, dark only. Every flight, hotel and time is made up. Everything here comes from the trips saved on the phone: no request goes to the server.

Proposed, not signed off. The owner picks the intents, the phrases and the wording in chat on #820.

A

Next flight

Spoken: the flight number, the day and time, and the zone's name. On the card: the route as airport codes, the booked wall-clock times with each airport's zone, the trip and how old the saved copy is. Never a confirmation code, gate, seat or traveller's name. Tap opens the flight in the app.

Spoken

  • Your next flight, EA 214, leaves Thursday at 7:40 am, Denver time. The day is today, tomorrow or the weekday within a week, else the date.
  • Today adds in 3 hours (or in 40 minutes).
  • The zone's name comes from the departure's IANA zone (America/Denver → Denver). Never converted to the phone's zone.

Card

  • Eyebrow Next flight · Thu Jun 12, the flight number, a chip: Booked times, or In 3 h on the day.
  • Codes in Geist Mono 28 px; times 13 px with the zone abbreviation of each airport.
  • Footer: the trip's name, and Saved 2 hours ago from the copy's savedAt.

Not shown

  • Confirmation code, gate, terminal, seat, cabin, travellers, live status.
  • City names: the saved copy has airport codes only (open question 5).
B

Tonight's stay, and check-out

One intent behind two phrases. Spoken: the hotel's name and city, and the check-out day and time. On the card, never spoken: the street address. Never a room number, confirmation code or phone number.

Rules

  • Tonight is today in the hotel's own zone; the stay covers it when check-in is on or before today and check-out after it.
  • No stay tonight: You have no stay tonight. Your next stay is Hotel Example, from Thursday. with the same card.
  • Times are the hotel's wall-clock times.

Card

  • Eyebrow Tonight (or Check-out Sunday), the hotel's name, a chip Night 2 of 3.
  • The address from details.address; check-in and check-out; footer with the zone and the copy's age.
C

In the Shortcuts app

Two actions, no more. They show under Waypoint in Shortcuts and in Spotlight as actions; no trip is ever indexed.

Why two

  • The owner doesn't want clutter: check-out is a phrase of the stay intent, not a third tile.
  • Parking, open my trip and live status are left out of the first set (see the write-up).
