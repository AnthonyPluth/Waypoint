# Bundled travel logos

The catalog in `manifest.json` records each brand, its local filename, source URL and upstream Git blob SHA. The app shows each brand's file in `marks/`: the symbol cut from its logo where it has one, otherwise the whole logo trimmed to its edges. The entry's `mark` is that cut, as fractions of the logo's width and height. SVG assets are static images with no scripts or external resources; PNGs are bundled as received, with a dark backing added to the white Regent mark so it stays visible. Brand marks remain the property of their respective owners and identify the providers of a booking or loyalty program.

Most SVGs come from [detain/svg-logos](https://github.com/detain/svg-logos), a mirror of WorldVectorLogo. The 33 brands whose marks are current (airlines and hotels listed in the manifest with `simple-icons` sources) come from [simple-icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) 16.35.0, whose icons are CC0; each is filled with its brand colour. Current logos for most of the other brands (airlines, hotel brands, car rental and cruise lines) come from Wikimedia Commons, where each is the logo that Wikidata records for the brand (property P154); the manifest entry names the Commons file and holds the SHA-1 of the file as downloaded. Others (Viking, DoubleTree, Crowne Plaza, Staybridge Suites, Days Inn and Windstar, and the eight that follow: Princess, Hilton, Seabourn, Holland America, Econo Lodge, China Airlines, Malaysia Airlines and Sonesta) come from the infobox logo of the brand’s English Wikipedia page, which shows a coloured current mark where the Wikidata one was black or grey. Two of the Commons files (Avis, Budget) were saved as UTF-16 with a UTF-8 declaration and are re-encoded as UTF-8, and the American Airlines symbol is recoloured to American's published brand colours (#0078D2, #C30019 and #9DA6AB) because the Commons file used other shades; the empty Adobe Illustrator `foreignObject` placeholder (which draws nothing and the catalogue test rejects) is removed from the Sleep Inn mark; and the Hyatt Regency PNG is converted from the CMYK JPEG in Hyatt's press kit. No other file is edited. Airline PNGs come from [sexym0nk3y/airline-logos](https://github.com/sexym0nk3y/airline-logos). Additional cruise SVGs come from [kaliartistry/cruisekit](https://github.com/kaliartistry/cruisekit). Hotel and additional cruise images come from the brand asset collections recorded in their entries. See each entry for its exact source.

The application reads these files from disk. It never contacts these sources at runtime. Add a reviewed static image and a manifest entry to extend coverage. Update matching aliases in `waypoint/storage/bundled_logos.py` when a provider uses another name.
